# EP-0007 — TASK-007 B31.3 prework

## Exact-source diagnosis

1. Preview material authority already prefers explicit/manual material, then line-list process material, and only falls back to piping-class material when process material is absent. B31.3 therefore is not generally blocked because line-list/piping-class material is ignored.
2. Preview B31.3 resolves that material to a governed material identity, then calls `resolveB313DesignFactors(... qualificationMode: 'strict')`.
3. Strict factor mode accepts only `VERIFIED` and `PROJECT_APPROVED`. The current project factor master exposes material identities for CS/LTCS/SS/DSS/SDSS, but its populated A106 S/E/W/Y records are `BENCHMARK_ONLY`; broader supplied rows are `SCREENING`. Those statuses are intentionally excluded from automatic design authority.
4. Material identity alone cannot safely generate E/Y/S/W. E can depend on manufacturing route/joint quality; S is material/temperature dependent; W and Y depend on governed code/material/temperature conditions. Line-list/piping-class material evidence can route identity, but it is not a numeric factor source.
5. Existing Preview failure codes are fail-closed but the row UI does not sufficiently explain rejected factor status/source or the complete input-authority chain, making a correct strict block look like a missing calculation.
6. Additional fail-closed boundaries include T1, positive P1, corrosion allowance, pressure unit, dimension standard, source-XML OD presence/parity against the selected pipe-wall master, calculation success, wall selection success, and a publishable wall override key.
