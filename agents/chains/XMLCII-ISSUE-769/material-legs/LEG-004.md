# LEG-004 receipt

## Root-cause result

- Preview material authority already consumes explicit/manual material, then line-list process material, then non-class material; piping-class material is the fallback when process material is absent.
- The B31.3 action resolves that Preview material to a governed material identity and then deliberately requests `qualificationMode: strict` design factors.
- Strict factor authority accepts only `VERIFIED` and `PROJECT_APPROVED`. The current runtime factor catalog contains material identities and candidate numeric factor rows, but the available populated factor records relevant to the supplied project data are `BENCHMARK_ONLY` or `SCREENING`. Those records are intentionally excluded from automatic design authority.
- Therefore line-list/piping-class data can establish material identity but cannot legitimately create S/E/W/Y. S is material/temperature dependent; E can additionally depend on manufacturing route/joint quality; W/Y remain code/material/temperature-controlled factors.
- Other fail-closed blockers remain positive P1, pressure unit, non-negative corrosion, dimension standard, source-XML OD presence/parity against the selected dimension master, B31.3 calculation success, pipe-wall selection success, and wall override-key publication.
