## 3. Protected engineering rules

- Preserve every original occurrence's lineage and raw XYZ. Topology-proven aliases at the same connected station may represent one physical vertex; disconnected co-located vertices remain distinct. No nearest, first, heaviest or reduced-tolerance tie breaker establishes that equivalence.
- Keep support occurrence, route carrier, attachment anchor, original/final XML occurrence and native node identities distinct. An ordinary support `REF` is not automatically a carrier reference.
- Geometry validates a source-supported relationship; projection alone cannot invent attachment authority. Any required split needs explicit engineering approval before Final acceptance and rebuilt lineage/mass evidence.
- One mandatory member resolves to one physical vertex; that vertex may receive 1–6 independently authored nodal restraint members on one canonical native node. Preserve REST, GUIDE and LINE STOP members, repeated kinds with distinct source occurrences, directions and explicit zero gaps separately.
- An inferred pre-Final restraint cannot independently corroborate its own owner. Preserve authored, inferred and generated evidence categories.
- Preserve existing mass authority, units, numerical tolerances, runtime/writer/canvas custody and failure guards. A failed `GOVERNED_FINAL` run cannot silently become `XML_BASE`.
- Keep raw source identifiers and the full census in approved private custody. Public diagnostics contain bounded source-safe evidence only.
- Missing or contradictory source authority remains HELD until a reviewed source correction or signed engineering decision creates new versioned evidence.
